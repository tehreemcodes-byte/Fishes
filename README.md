# Fishes
Fishes

Fishes are aquatic animals that live in water. They belong to the phylum Chordata and are mainly found in freshwater and marine environments.

Main Characteristics

- Fishes live in water.
- They breathe through gills.
- Their body is usually covered with scales.
- They have fins for movement and balance.
- They are cold-blooded animals.
- Most fishes lay eggs.
- They have a streamlined body.

Habitat

Fishes are found in different aquatic environments such as rivers, lakes, ponds, seas, and oceans.

Examples

- Goldfish
- Shark
- Salmon
- Tuna
- Catfish

Importance

Fishes are an important part of aquatic ecosystems. They are also an important source of food for humans and other animals.
